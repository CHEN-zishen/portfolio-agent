Surgi-Diff: A Diffusion-Based Generative Framework for Postoperative 
Outcome Prediction and Surgical Planning in Adolescent Idiopathic ScoliosisTitle of the paper
Research content
This research proposes Surgi-Diff, a diffusion generative framework integrated with a 
Spine Alignment Encoder, to generate anatomically consistent postoperative spinal X-
rays and predict implant structures for automated preoperative surgical planning and 
postoperative outcome prediction of adolescent idiopathic scoliosis.
Research results
Experiments on a cohort of 612 AIS patients show that Surgi-Diff achieves the highest 
SSIM among all comparison methods, accurately predicts fusion segments, rods and 
screw distributions, and generates postoperative X-rays highly consistent with real 
ground truth, verifying its clinical value for scoliosis surgical decision support.

?2Net: A Geometry-Guided Network for Accurate Spine Morphology 
Estimation from a Single RGB Image of Human BackTitle of the paper
Research content
This study proposes a two-stage geometry-guided G²Net for radiation-free spinal 
morphology estimation from single back RGB images of AIS patients. It first predicts 
precise back-surface depth via global-local feature fusion and Mamba modules, then 
leverages RGB-depth cues to train a LoRA-based conditional diffusion model to 
generate anatomically consistent spine curves. Experiments on a clinical dataset of 
2,213 adolescents validate its superior performance against state-of-the-art methods.
Research results
Experiments on 2,213 clinical samples demonstrate that G²Net surpasses mainstream 
comparison methods, achieving a spine curve SSIM of 0.950 with low shape errors. 
Ablation tests verify the necessity of all core modules.

Scoliosis with Morphology-Guided Latent Diffusion ModelTitle of the paper
Research content
This work designs ScolioLDM, which uses predicted spine contour maps and a 
MGCM to guide latent diffusion for high-fidelity AIS postoperative X-ray synthesis, 
validated on a 612-patient clinical dataset.
Research results
Experiments on 612 AIS cases prove ScolioLDM outperforms mainstream generative 
models, achieving top PSNR and NCC. Ablations verify spine contour maps and 
MGCM are vital for realistic spine and implant reconstruction.
